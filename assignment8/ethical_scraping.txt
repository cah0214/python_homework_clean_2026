Task 5: Ethical Scraping

Wikepedia does not allow bots to access certain parts of its websites
including /w/, /api/, /trap/, sepacial pages, and some administrative
or discussion pages. 

There are also different rules for certain bots. Some bots are compeletely
blocked from the website, while SemrushBot is told to wait five seconds between requests.

The robots.txt file helps prevent bots from overloading or misusing
a website. Ethical web scraping means following these rules, avoiding
restricted pages, and sending requets at a resonable speed.
